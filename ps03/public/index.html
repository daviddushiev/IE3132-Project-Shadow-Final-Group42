PS-03 — The Attacker's Message

Incident Update

During the investigation of the Solace Dynamics research portal, internal notes revealed that the attacker left behind an encoded message.

The message was intended to hide information from casual observers, but evidence recovered during the investigation may help determine how it was protected.

Objectives

Your task is to:

  • Identify the cipher used to encode the message.
  • Determine the correct key using contextual clues.
  • Decrypt the attacker's message.
  • Recover the Stage 3 flag.
  • Record any information required for the next stage.
